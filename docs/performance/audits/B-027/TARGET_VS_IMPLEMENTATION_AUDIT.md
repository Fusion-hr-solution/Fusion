# B-027 target versus implementation audit

## Scope and interpretation

This compares the refined Performance target and the approved `establish-performance-target-state` OpenSpec with the inspected working tree. The implementation analysis and apply notes are supporting history, not proof. “Partial” means some current code paths were inspected but the full contract was not independently demonstrated. It does not mean the feature is known to be broken.

The change adds formal Review rounds and assessments, optional Calibration, Update Requests, and versioned review definitions. It also extends existing Cycle, planning, objective, progress/evidence, Team Performance, Overview, and Settings areas. Their presence in the changed checkout does not make each entire surface new to this change.

## Net conclusion

The approved change introduces a substantial Reviews workflow, optional Calibration, Update Requests and versioned assessment definitions on top of pre-existing Cycle, plan, objective, progress and Team surfaces. The highest-risk problem is not visual quality: calibration and review projections violate the approved “subjects never see calibration detail / manager content before release” boundary when one account is both subject and facilitator. Notification retries also do not meet the current no-duplicate delivery contract. The change is **not ready to archive** until the privacy boundary is corrected and the notification contract is either met or explicitly amended.
